I use a centered firing policy that seeks the shallowest detectable diver below row 1, avoids dangerous lanes, and returns with a diver when oxygen is low. An emergency ascent overrides conservative avoidance near oxygen exhaustion.

The first layer detects lane hazards, divers, position, gauges, and bullets. The second combines these into movement risks, target directions, centering corrections, and surfacing safeguards.
