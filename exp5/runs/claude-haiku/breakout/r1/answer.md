Design (hand-set, no training).

Strategy: the ball channel and the trail channel each hold exactly one cell, and the paddle is the only lit cell in row 9 of channel 0, so the ball column bx, ball row by, previous column lx, previous row ly and paddle column p are all linear functions of the input (weight = column index or row index). The ball's direction is ball minus trail.
- Descending down-right (dx=+1, dy=+1): the ball reaches row 8 after k = 8 - by steps at unfolded column u = bx - by + 8. With one wall fold the landing target is T = min(u, 18 - u). Move right if T > p, left if T < p, otherwise stay.
- Descending down-left (dx=-1, dy=+1): u = bx + by - 8 and the target is T = |u|.
- Ascending or horizontal: track the ball column (move toward bx).
- Catch window: the paddle at T is inside the 2-cell window the ball needs, so a correct target catches the ball.

Hidden layer 1 (12 units, tanh(3*(v - threshold)) threshold features, scale 3 on every coefficient):
  h1[0]  [u - p >= 1] (down-right)        h1[1]  [p - u >= 1] (down-right)
  h1[2]  [u + p >= 18] (down-right)       h1[3]  [u + p >= 19] (down-right)
  h1[4]  [u - p >= 1] (down-left)         h1[5]  [p - u >= 1] (down-left)
  h1[6]  [u + p >= 0] (down-left)         h1[7]  [u + p >= 1] (down-left)
  h1[8]  gate: down-right (dx+dy = 2)     h1[9]  gate: down-left (-dx+dy = 2)
  h1[10] [bx - p >= 1]                    h1[11] [p - bx >= 1]
Hidden layer 2 (8 AND units, weight 4 on each input, bias -4*(n-1)*0.9):
  h2[0] right: down-right and u-p>=1 and u+p<=17
  h2[1] left:  down-right and p-u>=1
  h2[2] left:  down-right and u+p>=19
  h2[3] right: down-left and u-p>=1
  h2[4] right: down-left and u+p<=-1 (wall fold)
  h2[5] left:  down-left and p-u>=1 and u+p>=1
  h2[6] right: not descending and bx>p
  h2[7] left:  not descending and p>bx
Output: logit right (action 3) = 10*(h2[0]+h2[3]+h2[4]+h2[6]) + 40, logit left (action 1) = 10*(h2[1]+h2[2]+h2[5]+h2[7]) + 40. Actions 0, 2, 4, 5 (all no-ops) share logit 5. When nothing fires, left and right are 0 and no-op wins; when one fires its logit is about 20 against 5, so the policy is close to deterministic.

Known limits: the wall stall costs one cell, which the window tolerates only approximately; the ascent tracking can lag; the policy is unverified because nothing can be run.
